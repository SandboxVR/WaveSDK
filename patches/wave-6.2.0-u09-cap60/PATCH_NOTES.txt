Limits and interpretation
  This fixes the tested zero-return prediction failure; it does not establish
  lower physical latency or resolve all perceived stuttering. The headset was
  stationary with the proximity sensor covered. Motion quality remains untested.
  First patched trial reused4.97% of images, second0.045%; controls0.27%/0.030%.
  The higher reuse did not repeat consistently and its cause remains unproven.
  One stale prediction occurred in the first patched run, zero in the second.
  Latency averages cannot be compared directly because controls omit failing
  samples. Restored measurements also affect SSVR's prediction feedback.
  The60ms cap is an experimental bound for the tested75/90Hz paths, not a
  universal refresh-rate policy. Live prediction horizons reached56.285ms.
  The initial control launch failed refresh-rate readiness checks. A later
  patched launch also exited before capture, with its reason not separately
  established. Unchanged retries succeeded; no startup fix was included.
  Nested APK signing metadata no longer authenticates the modified module;
  this is an application asset, not an installable signed system-provider APK.
