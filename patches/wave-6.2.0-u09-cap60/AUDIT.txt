Safety implications:
Estimator also serves WaitGetPoses/GetSyncPose (u25 function0x5e8740, call0x5e879c) and ApplyPoseLatency (u25 function0x5e89d0, call0x5e8a1c). Raising cap changes actual pose horizons, not just statistics. Prediction above50ms could remain wrong if timing model or vsync measurement is wrong. Motion accuracy and smoothness require live validation; restoring measurements does not prove stutter fixed.

Recommendation:
Prefer a bounded experimental override through existing registry, scoped to SSVR and75Hz. Keep the clipping/error guard at a tested higher bound, e.g.60ms leaves margin above53.333ms. Preserve default50ms at90Hz unless evidence justifies otherwise. Do not bypass the getter error branch (would hide clipping) or reduce pipeline periods without evidence (could underpredict pose).
If no official setter is accessible, exact binary identity and code/data fingerprints could gate an in-process invocation of the existing private parser. This avoids replacing the system runtime, but remains private ABI: use default-off feature flag, bounded constant string, readback, rollback and device validation; fail closed on unknown build. Do not call before registry initialization.
